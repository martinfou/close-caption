The Problem & The Fix

Why has nobody solved this on mobile?

On Mac and PC, Chrome extensions let you translate subtitles. But on iPad and iPhone, browsers and video apps are locked down. Apple’s built-in Live Captions only transcribe words—they don’t translate them into your language.

🪟

Universal Floating PiP

Using native iOS Picture-in-Picture, your subtitles float seamlessly on top of YouTube, VLC, Crunchyroll, or Safari. Drag it anywhere or resize on the fly.

🛡️

100% Private & On-Device

Powered by Apple’s Neural Engine and offline language packs. Not a single byte of your audio is uploaded to the cloud or stored on external servers.

Intelligent Reading Dwell

Fast dialogues don’t vanish in a blink. LiveSub’s configurable reading buffer holds each translated sentence steady so you finish reading comfortably.
