The Problem & The Fix

Why has nobody solved this on mobile?

On Mac and PC, Chrome extensions let you translate subtitles. But on mobile devices (iOS and Android), video apps and streaming browsers are locked down. Standard live captions only transcribe spoken words—they don’t translate them into your native language.
