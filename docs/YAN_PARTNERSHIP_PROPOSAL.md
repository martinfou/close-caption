## 3. The Proposed Agreement: 15% Net Profit Share

We propose bringing you on as LiveSub's **Founding Product Advisor & Lead QA Tester** with a **15% Net Profit Share**.

### How It Works:
* **15% of Net Profit:** You receive **15% of all net cash profit** generated by LiveSub across all platforms (Apple App Store subscriptions, Google Play in-app purchases, and direct website APK sales).
* **Payout Schedule:** Distributed **quarterly** (every 3 months) via direct transfer (Zelle, Venmo, or bank wire) alongside a simple transparent financial statement.
* **What "Net Profit" Means:** Gross revenue minus direct platform taxes and fees (Apple/Google 15% store commission, Stripe payment processing fees, refunds/chargebacks, and direct annual developer licenses). There are no bloated corporate expenses or fake deductions—our server cost is literally **$0.00** because inference runs on the user's phone.

### What This Looks Like in Real Numbers (From Our Business Plan):
* **At 350 Subscribers ($1,500/mo net profit):** Your share is **~$225 / month** (~$675/quarter) for watching shows and testing builds.
